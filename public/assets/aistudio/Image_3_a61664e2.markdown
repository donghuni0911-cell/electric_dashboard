## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an approachable, high-trust Korean energy market electricity price dashboard on mobile web. The brand personality balances financial clarity, institutional dependability, and approachable consumer guidance. It demystifies fluctuating spot prices (SMP - System Marginal Price, REC - Renewable Energy Certificate) for newcomers, solar micro-generators, and general power consumers.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern FinTech** with **Soft Utility Minimalism**:
- **Clarity Over Complexity:** Technical market metrics are translated into intuitive visual statuses (e.g., "Low / Stable / High" color-coded badges) alongside numeric figures.
- **Airy, Crisp Canvas:** Expansive clean surfaces reduce visual noise and fatigue.
- **Electric Precision:** Vivid electric blue accents draw the eye to critical real-time action items and active tracking curves without feeling alarming.
- **Friendly Financial Trust:** Generous card padding, gentle edge curvature, and clean typographic rhythms invite exploration without intimidation.

## Colors

The color palette centers on crisp light surfaces with high-energy electric blues that signal vitality, stability, and technological speed:

- **Primary (`#1d4ed8`)**: Royal Electric Blue used for key price indicators, active interactive states, primary action buttons, and dominant graph trends.
- **Secondary (`#0ea5e9`)**: Sky Blue used for informational callouts, comparative benchmark charts, and secondary selection badges.
- **Tertiary (`#10b981`)**: Renewable Green indicating solar generation surpluses, off-peak savings, and eco-positive market trends.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`)**: Slate gray scale providing balanced typography hierarchy and subtle UI boundaries.
- **Canvas Base**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) for foreground cards and sheet surfaces; ultra-soft slate (`#f8fafc`) for base page backdrops.
- **Semantic Accents**: Amber (`#f59e0b`) for moderate price surges; Coral Red (`#ef4444`) strictly reserved for peak price alerts and critical system warnings.

## Typography

The typography system relies on **Inter** (pairing smoothly with Pretendard in multi-language Korean/English environments) to provide clean geometric construction and high legibility across mobile screens:

- **Tabular Figures:** Numbers in pricing cards, hourly SMP charts, and percentage differences must employ tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifts during live data ticks.
- **Korean Typographic Balance:** Korean headers (한글 제목) use tighter letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain cohesion and prevent loose spacing artifacts.
- **Hierarchy:** High-impact metric summaries use `stat-display` for instant grasp of prices per kilowatt-hour (원/kWh), paired directly with `label-md` unit tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, targeting modern mobile viewport widths (360px–430px) while adapting cleanly to tablet dashboards:

- **Mobile Viewport Grid:** A 4-column fluid layout with an outer horizontal margin of `1rem` (16px) and column gutters of `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Tablet & Expandable Views:** Scales to 8 columns at 768px with `1.5rem` margins, enabling side-by-side market comparison charts.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component interiors adhere to an 8px scale. Dense data rows within cards use `space-sm` (8px), while distinct dashboard metric widgets use `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px) separation to avoid cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs **Low-Contrast Outlines paired with Soft Ambient Tonal Elevation**, avoiding harsh shadows in favor of crisp daylight clarity:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Background canvas rendered in `#f8fafc`.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** Solid `#ffffff` background with a subtle border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`). Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Menus):** Pure white background with border (`1px solid #cbd5e1`) and an ambient diffused shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Snackbars):** Elevated bottom sheets use `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)` over a semi-transparent dark backdrop blur (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with 4px backdrop blur).

## Shapes

The design uses **Roundedness Level 2 (Rounded)**:
- Standard elements (buttons, inputs, metric cells): `0.5rem` (8px).
- Surface cards and interactive modular panels: `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px).
- Floating action buttons, bottom utility sheets, and primary spotlight banners: `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem` (24px).
- Pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) are strictly used for market status chips, time interval toggles (1H, 24H, 7D, 1M), and trend indicator pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1d4ed8`, text `#ffffff`, border-radius `0.5rem`, height `48px` (mobile touch target). Subtle hover/active brightness shift to `#1e40af`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** White background, `1px solid #cbd5e1`, text `#1e293b`.
- **Icon Buttons:** `40x40px` touch areas with centered iconography for alerts, info popups, and refreshing rates.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`) with light tonal backgrounds. 
  - Off-peak / Cheap: `#ecfdf5` background, `#059669` text.
  - Normal / Mid: `#eff6ff` background, `#1d4ed8` text.
  - Peak / Expensive: `#fef2f2` background, `#dc2626` text.
- **Time-Range Switcher:** Segmented control container with `#f1f5f9` fill and `0.5rem` rounded corners. Active state features a pure white pill with soft elevation.

### Cards
- **Market Metric Card:** Features a top row with label and explanation tooltip icon, a prominent price figure (`stat-display`), and a percentage delta badge showing movement compared to yesterday's average.
- **Hourly Price Curve Card:** White canvas housing an interactive lightweight SVG/Canvas sparkline chart, touch scrub bar, and current selected-hour floating readout.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height `48px`, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, focus border `2px solid #1d4ed8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20x20px` with `#1d4ed8` fill on checked states and white checkmark icon.

### Price Alert Banner (Beginner Feature)
- Dedicated banner explaining current price context in simple conversational Korean ("현재 시간대는 전력 수요가 낮아 평소보다 18% 저렴합니다"). Soft electric-blue tint background (`#eff6ff`), electric blue left border accent (4px), and clear body text.
